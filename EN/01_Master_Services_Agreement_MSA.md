## 2. RECITALS & DEFINITIONS

* **"FinGuard AI" / "Platform" / "Software":** The proprietary cloud-native, artificial intelligence-powered software-as-a-service platform developed by Vendor for real-time financial fraud detection, anomaly monitoring, and automated credit risk scoring.
* **"Bank Data":** Any and all data, transactions, customer records, metadata, personal data, and business secrets uploaded, ingested, processed, or generated by or on behalf of the Bank through the Platform.
* **"Banking Secrecy":** Information classified as banking or customer secret under Article 73 of Turkish Banking Law No. 5411, European Banking Authority (EBA) Outsourcing Guidelines, and associated financial secrecy regulations.
* **"Regulatory Authority":** The Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency of Türkiye (BRSA / BDDK), Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye (CBRT / TCMB), European Central Bank (ECB), or any competent supervisory governmental body.
* **"Schedules":** The integral attachments to this Agreement: Schedule A (SLA & SLO), Schedule B (Statement of Work & Technical Specs), Schedule C (Data Processing Agreement - DPA), Schedule D (Information Security & Cyber Resilience), and Schedule E (Business Continuity, Escrow & Exit Strategy).

## 3. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the legal, commercial, and technical framework under which Vendor provides the Bank with subscription access to the FinGuard AI Platform hosted in a dedicated, isolated Virtual Private Cloud (VPC), alongside integration, custom development, technical support, and disaster recovery services.

---

## 4. SUBSCRIPTION GRANT AND USAGE RIGHTS

4.1. **License Grant:** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Vendor grants to the Bank and its direct and indirect affiliates a non-exclusive, worldwide, non-transferable (except as permitted under Section 10), and irrevocable (for the agreed term) right to access and use the Platform for internal banking and business operations.  
4.2. **Authorized Scope:** The Bank may execute transactions, connect its Core Banking systems via authorized APIs, and permit its employees, contractors, and auditors to access the Platform up to the transaction volumes and limits specified in Schedule B.  
4.3. **Reverse Engineering & Restrictions:** Except as expressly set forth herein or in Schedule E (Software Escrow), the Bank shall not reverse engineer, decompile, or create derivative works from the Platform's core proprietary source code.

---

## 5. COVENANTS AND OBLIGATIONS

### 5.1. Vendor's Covenants:
a) Ensure the Platform meets or exceeds the **99.95% monthly availability commitment** as defined in Schedule A (SLA/SLO).  
b) Strictly store and process all Bank Data within sovereign data centers located inside the Republic of Türkiye, complying with BRSA data localization and residency mandates.  
c) Complete system integrations and milestone deliverables in accordance with the specifications in Schedule B (SoW).  
d) Maintain continuous compliance with ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and PCI-DSS Level 1 standards as mandated in Schedule D.

### 5.2. Bank's Covenants:
a) Pay undisputed fees in accordance with Section 6 and Schedule B.  
b) Provide reasonable cooperation, test credentials, and architectural specifications required for core banking API connectivity.  
c) Maintain appropriate security over authentication credentials and API cryptographic tokens under its direct control.

## 6. COMMERCIAL TERMS, INVOICING, AND PAYMENT

6.1. **Fees:** The Bank shall pay the subscription, integration, and volume-based usage fees outlined in Schedule B.  
6.2. **Invoicing and Payment Term:** Vendor shall issue an itemized electronic invoice on the last business day of each calendar month, reflecting any applicable Service Credits incurred during that month. The Bank shall pay undisputed invoices within **thirty (30) calendar days** of receipt via wire transfer.  
6.3. **Taxes:** All stated prices are exclusive of VAT. Any withholding taxes required by applicable tax legislation shall be deducted by the Bank and remitted to the appropriate tax authorities on behalf of Vendor.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS & INDEMNIFICATION

7.1. **Background IP:** Vendor retains exclusive ownership of all pre-existing intellectual property, patents, algorithms, core software code, and documentation comprising the FinGuard AI Platform.  
7.2. **Bank Ownership:** The Bank exclusively owns all Bank Data, Bank-specific custom rule sets, proprietary data schemas, and machine learning models trained or fine-tuned using Bank Data.  
7.3. **IP Infringement Indemnification:** Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Bank, its officers, and employees against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform or the Bank's authorized use thereof infringes any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret. Vendor shall pay all awarded damages, settlement amounts, reasonable legal fees, and court costs.

---

## 8. REGULATORY OVERSIGHT AND AUDIT RIGHTS

8.1. **Statutory Audit Rights:** Vendor acknowledges that the Bank is subject to intense regulatory oversight. Vendor hereby grants the Bank, its internal and external auditors, and statutory regulatory bodies (including BRSA and CBRT) **unrestricted on-site inspection and audit rights** over the hosting facilities, operations, systems, data stores, logs, and processes utilized for the Platform.  
8.2. **Audit Cooperation:** Vendor shall provide all necessary technical documentation, access logs, compliance attestations, and staff interviews within **three (3) business days** of an audit request.

---

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1. **General Liability Cap:** Except as provided in Section 9.2, each Party's aggregate total liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be limited to the total fees paid or payable by the Bank to Vendor in the twelve (12) months preceding the incident.  
9.2. **Uncapped Liability (Carve-outs):** The liability limitation under Section 9.1 **shall NOT apply** to:  
a) Breach of Section 7.3 (IP Indemnification),  
b) Breach of Banking Secrecy, confidentiality obligations, or Schedule C (Data Processing Agreement / KVKK / GDPR),  
c) Administrative fines imposed on the Bank by regulatory authorities (BRSA, KVKK) resulting from Vendor's breach or security failure,  
d) Gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud.

---

## 10. TERM, TERMINATION, AND TRANSITION

10.1. **Term:** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and remains in effect for an initial term of **three (3) years**, automatically renewing for successive one (1) year periods unless terminated by either Party upon sixty (60) days' written notice.  
10.2. **Termination for Cause:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days of receipt of written notice.  
10.3. **Regulatory Mandate:** The Bank may terminate this Agreement immediately without penalty if any Regulatory Authority orders the suspension or cessation of the outsourcing relationship.  
10.4. **Exit Assistance:** Upon termination for any reason, Vendor shall provide up to six (6) months of uninterrupted transition support pursuant to Schedule E.
